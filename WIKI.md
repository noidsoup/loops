The wiki lives in **`wiki/`** at the repo root (until 2026-10-19 it was `loops wiki/`).

- Start at `wiki/index.md`. Writing rules: `wiki/SCHEMA.md`.
- In Obsidian: *Open folder as vault* → choose `wiki/`.
- Session breadcrumbs stay in `AI_SESSION_MEMORY.md`; the wiki holds compiled knowledge (decisions, gotchas, how parts fit).
